Chrome states

Drive the real chrome.

These three controls operate the actual header above, not a picture of it. The scrolled state tightens both rows and lifts the bar onto a shadow; the triage panel opens under the first destination; the drawer is the same one a phone gets.

The mobile drawer, open, rendered at a true 390px viewport. This is the file rendering itself in a frame, so what is shown is the real breakpoint behaviour and not a mock-up of it.

Why the bar collapses at 1140px

Measured in a headless render of this file, not estimated. The eight labels occupy 847px on one line and the wordmark 175px, which with the 20px row gap is 1042px against the 1092px the shared container gives. That is 50px of slack, so the cluster holds one line at every width down to the collapse. Putting the booking CTA at the end of the cluster instead needed 1286px, which does not fit the container at ANY width: the row wrapped to two lines even at 1440 and the wrap hid it from the overflow check. The CTA therefore lives in the strip. Below 1140px the bar collapses to the drawer, so the tablet band at 768, 834 and 1024 is served by the drawer rather than by an overflowing row.
