Brand card C of 3

EVIDENCE: the structure is the hero

This practice competes on being checkable. Its real differentiator is the estimate written before the examination and the narrative report an attorney can read, so the design language is documentary: a visible measured structure, ruled columns, tabular alignment, mono data accents, and framing that reads like a record rather than an advertisement. The measured signature hero is dark and there is no photograph to carry it, so the dark hero is built as a dark DOCUMENT: a ruled field with a tab cut into its top edge, corner registration marks and a real grain, never an image slot waiting for a picture that will not arrive.

Type: Space Grotesk over Inter (TYPOGRAPHY-PAIRINGS.md #39), with Space Mono bound to the mono token for every label, figure and reference. Palette anchored on the practice's own logo, which C7 §2 records as the only mark the practice owns.

05 / Type scale

Display / Space Grotesk 700 / tracking -0.03em

The price in writing, before we examine you

Body / Inter 400 / leading 1.62

Long form reading is the load bearing job. The terms block, the thirteen item FAQ and five condition pages all have to survive sustained reading by someone in pain, often on a phone, often at night. Inter is here to be unremarkable at 17px and to stay legible reversed out of the dark document.

Data / Space Mono 700 / uppercase / tracking 0.08em

The mono face is the documentary register. It is reserved for labels, figures, references and form rows, so a number always looks like a number and never like a headline.
