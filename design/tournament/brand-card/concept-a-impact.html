IMPACT: the type is the hero

The brand's measured signature is a full-bleed dark hero with large display type over a photograph. That photograph is stock and cannot ship. This concept keeps the energy and drops the photo: the hero is built from type scale, a measured grid material and pure contrast, so it never needs an image, and it cannot quietly decay back into a stock-photo look.

Type: Archivo Black over Archivo (typography menu #35). Palette anchored on the practice's own logo rather than a library family, because C7 records it as the only mark the practice owns.
