DEPTH: the dark surface is a lit material

The brand's measured signature is a full bleed dark hero, and the photograph that used to carry it is stock, so it cannot ship. Concept B answers that with material rather than with type scale: the dark plane is built from a directional base gradient, a soft bloom in the practice's own cyan falling from the top left, a grain veil at the faint opacity token, and real elevation between planes, so it reads as a lit physical surface that was designed on purpose, never as a black rectangle or an empty slot waiting for a picture.

Type is Source Serif 4 over Inter, typography menu 43. The serif is the point: this practice's differentiator is a document, the estimate it puts in writing before it examines you, and a serif gives that promise an authority a grotesque cannot. Inter carries every number, label and control.

The material set: what the dark surface is made of

Layer 1, the base

A 158 degree gradient across three dark tokens, so the plane is lit from one corner instead of filled flat.

Layer 2, the bloom

Two radial washes in the practice's cyan and deep blue, held at the texture opacity token, off the top left corner.

Layer 3, the grain

A token-built noise veil at the faint opacity token. No asset, no file to source, and nothing that can decay into a stock wash.

The elevation ladder

Three planes, three shadows: paper at rest, the panel lifted, the glass plane floating. Every shadow is ink tinted, never pure black.
