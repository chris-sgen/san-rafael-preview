What these three fragments lock

Figure and ground

Three declared grounds, walked in order: lit dark material, calm paper, sunken. The dark is the figure and carries roughly the measured share of the page, never a slot for a photograph.

Type scale applied

Four steps do all the work: hero, band, card, reading. The serif carries the promise, the sans carries every number, label and control.

Section rhythm

One band token of air between fragments, one curve seam out of the dark, one numbered hairline into the sunken row. Two seam kinds, never three.

Motion budget

One entrance per band, a 70ms stagger across the card row, a colour shift plus a 5px arrow nudge on the primary action, a 4px card lift. Nothing loops and nothing moves without intent.

Surface treatment

Base gradient, radial bloom, grain veil, contrast scrim, four declared elevations, hairline borders and inset top highlights. Every value resolves through the locked token sheet.

Reduced motion

Every transition and animation is zeroed and every revealed element is forced visible. The reveal start state is a keyframe, never a base style, so no band can be left hidden if the motion does not run.

The three grounds above are the figure/ground decision in one strip: the material, the reading canvas, the recessed row. If a future band cannot be built out of these three plus the two seams, it is a new decision and it comes back here rather than being improvised on a page.
