The paperwork, before you get here

Six forms, filled in at your own kitchen table, which is a better place to write about a car accident than a clipboard in a waiting room.

Print them and bring them in, or fill them in here when you arrive.

The forms, and what each one is for

1. New patient health history. Prior injuries, current medications, and any imaging you have already had.

This is the form that lets the doctor separate what the collision caused from what was there before it, which is the single most contested point in any injury claim. Being thorough here is in your interest, not ours.

2. Collision detail form. Date, time and location. Direction of impact. Where you were sitting, whether you were belted, whether the airbags deployed. Whether you were seen at an emergency department. And when your symptoms started.

That last field matters more than it looks. Delayed onset is normal after a collision: people routinely feel fine at the scene and stiff two days later. The form is built to record that honestly rather than treat "I felt fine that day" as an admission against you.

3. Insurance and claim information. Your carrier, the claim number, the adjuster, your medical payments coverage if you have it, and the other driver's carrier.

Answer as far as you know. Blank fields here are expected, and we verify the rest.

4. Attorney information (optional). The firm, the contact there, and whether we may communicate with them about your care.

If you do not have an attorney, leave it blank. Having one is not a condition of being treated here.

5. Authorisation to release records. Who may receive your examination findings, and on what basis.

Nothing goes to an insurer, an attorney or anyone else without this.

6. Financial responsibility acknowledgment. The money terms, in writing, signed.

It says the same thing the rest of the site says: care can be provided on a lien, you are billed nothing while your case is open, and we are paid from the settlement. If we cannot accept the case, if the case is lost, or if it settles for less than the bill, the balance is written off in full and you owe nothing. All five funding routes are set out on cost.

How to complete and return them

Print the set and bring it with you, or arrive fifteen minutes before your appointment and complete it here.

Either is fine and neither is better.

If writing hurts, say so.

A neck, shoulder or hand injury makes filling in six forms genuinely difficult, and somebody at the desk can take the answers down for you. This is a normal request after a collision and nobody will think anything of it.

If you do not have your claim number yet, come anyway and bring it later.

Paperwork is not a reason to postpone the examination, and the missing field can be filled in at any point.

One honest note on submitting online.

The current forms are downloads.

Until this practice controls its own submission route end to end, we are not going to promise that a form sent through a website will be sitting on the doctor's desk when you arrive.

Bringing the paper is the version we can stand behind.

Why doing this before the visit is worth it

Two reasons, and both of them are about you.

The appointment is finite.

Every minute you spend writing your address and your date of birth is a minute not spent on the examination, which is the part you actually came for. Paperwork done in advance turns a visit into an examination rather than an intake session.

The second reason is the one people do not think of.

What you write down about the crash while you still remember it clearly, which way you were facing, whether your head hit the restraint, when the stiffness started, is what the examination findings are later read against. That detail is not recoverable months afterwards; it gets reconstructed, and a reconstruction is worth less than a contemporaneous note. An auto-accident injury usually needs looking at sooner rather than later, and the record of it starts with what you write here.

The gap between the collision and the first documented examination is read in exactly this way by the people assessing the claim. Nothing on this page can make that gap shorter. Filling the forms in early just means the record starts with accurate facts instead of a rushed guess.
