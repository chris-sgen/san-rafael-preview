What was on this page before, and what replaces it

This page was rebuilt in 2026. The version it replaces carried patient stories about a serious illness, about a childhood condition and school results, about a neurological condition, and about coming off medication, plus a line about how many people the practice has helped and a set of testimonials that all shared one stock photograph of a person who was not a patient here.

None of that could be traced to something you can check, and some of it made claims a chiropractic practice has no business making. When the practice reviewed them, the decision was to drop all of them. They are gone from the whole site, not reworded and moved elsewhere.

One more thing belongs here rather than in a footnote. In 2025 the practice replied in public to a critical review and accepted that its old website wording about the first conversation was unclear. That wording is gone as well. What replaces it is on the cost page: care runs on a lien, you are billed nothing while your case is open, and the practice is paid from the settlement. If the case is not accepted, is lost, or settles for less than the bill, the balance is written off in full and you owe nothing.

The rules this page follows

No rating figure and no review total.

Not on screen and not in this site's structured data. A number on a page that nothing else can confirm is not proof, and a practice that publishes a headline figure its own data contradicts has taught its readers to discount every other number on the site. We would rather print no figure than print one you cannot reconcile.

Nothing is added up.

The listings are separate and they stay separate. A duplicate Google listing for the parent practice holds further reviews and splits the pool, and Yelp holds back some reviews it does not currently recommend. You would find both of those on your own, and being told afterwards is being misled by omission.

The practice's own two disclaimers stay.

They were on the old site, they are honest, and a cleaner design is not a reason to quietly drop them:

Results are not guaranteed and may vary from person to person.

Testimonials presented apply only to the individuals depicted, cannot be guaranteed, and should not be considered typical.

What you will not find here:

a count of people helped, a promise about your recovery, a claim about what your insurer will do, or a superlative about this practice.
