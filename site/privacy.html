Privacy policy

Who publishes this policy. San Rafael Auto Accident Injury Chiropractic Center, Dr. Michael Perry Joseph, D.C., an auto-injury practice of Pain Relief Clinic of Marin, 55 Mitchell Blvd, Ste 10, San Rafael, CA 94903, (415) 877-9400.

What it covers. This website, and the information you send through it or give over the phone in response to it. The practice operates under two names at one address, and the two names are one practice: which of them is named as the formal record holder in your patient file is being settled with the practice's counsel, and this line will name it once it is settled.

Effective date. This policy takes effect on the day this site goes live, and that date is printed here when it does.

Last reviewed. 2026-08-20.

What this policy is not. It is not a notice of privacy practices for your clinical records. That is a separate document, it is given to you at the practice, and it governs your treatment file rather than this website.

The policy

3.1 What we collect when you submit a form

Plain answer
You decide what it gets, and you can send nothing at all. The fields are your name, your phone number, an email address, the date of your collision, a description of your injury and, if you have them, your insurance and your attorney's details. Every one of them can be left blank, and you can call the practice instead.
The clause itself

The consultation form on this site asks for your name, your phone number, an email address, the date of your collision, a description of your injury, and, if you have them, your insurance details and your attorney's details. You choose what to put in it. Nothing on the form is needed to be examined: you can leave every field blank and call instead.

3.2 What we collect automatically

Plain answer
Server logs, and the practice cannot yet tell you what else. The logs record the address your request came from, the page you asked for and the time. A tag manager container also runs here, and which tags it loads, and whether any of them run before you consent, is still being confirmed. Until it is, this policy does not tell you that none of them do.
The clause itself

Ordinary web server logs, which record the address your request came from, the page you asked for and the time. A tag manager container also runs on this site, which is a piece of software that can load analytics and advertising code.

Stated plainly because you deserve better than a vague sentence here: the practice is still confirming which tags that container actually loads, and whether any of them run before you consent. Until that inventory is confirmed, this policy does not tell you that none of them do. When it is confirmed, the list is published in this section and this paragraph is replaced by it.

3.3 What we collect when you call

Plain answer
What you say on the call, and the call is not recorded. That is your name, a number to call you back on, and enough about the collision and the injury to book the right appointment.
The clause itself

What you tell us: your name, a number to call you back on, and enough about the collision and the injury to book the right appointment. Calls are not recorded.

3.4 Health information, and how it is treated differently

Plain answer
Your clinical record is held in the practice's patient records rather than in the website's mailbox, and which regulations attach to which of them is a question the practice has put to counsel rather than answered from guesswork. A form sent by someone who has not been examined is an enquiry, not a clinical record. The practical rule the practice works to is that injury details you send through this site are treated as confidential and are not used for advertising.
The clause itself

If you become a patient, the notes taken at your examination, the diagnosis, the treatment and any imaging are part of your clinical record. They are kept in the practice's patient records, not in the website's mailbox, and they are released only as described in 3.5.

A form submission from someone who has not yet been examined is an enquiry, not a clinical record. Which regulations attach to which of those two categories for this practice is a question for counsel and not one this page answers from guesswork. What we can tell you is the practical rule the practice works to: injury details you send through this site are treated as confidential and are not used for advertising.

3.5 Who we share it with, and on what authority

Plain answer
No, the practice does not sell it, and nothing goes to your attorney, your insurer or another provider until you have signed an authorisation telling us to send it. You can withdraw that authorisation by telling us in writing. No injury details go to a lawyer referral service or to any other business that would market to you, and this practice pays and receives no referral fees.
The clause itself

Your records go to your attorney, your insurer, or another provider when you have signed an authorisation telling us to send them, and not before. The release form is on the forms page, and you can withdraw the authorisation by telling us in writing.

The practice does not sell your information, and does not pass your injury details to a lawyer referral service or to any other business that would market to you. This practice pays and receives no referral fees.

3.6 Companies that process it for us

Plain answer
An outside vendor already receives what you type into the form, and the practice cannot yet name it. It is still establishing which company controls that endpoint, the submissions already held in it, and the tag manager container, and it will not name a company it does not actually contract with. If you would rather your crash details did not pass through a third party's system, phone the practice instead of using the form.
The clause itself

The consultation form on this site is supplied and hosted by an outside vendor, which means what you type into it reaches their system as well as ours. The practice is in the middle of establishing which company controls that endpoint, the historic submissions held in it, and the tag manager container. No processor is named in this policy until that is settled, because naming a company the practice does not actually contract with would be a false statement in a legal document. Until it is settled, the honest advice is this: if you would rather your crash details did not pass through a third party's system, phone the practice on (415) 877-9400 instead of using the form.

3.7 How long we keep it

Plain answer
You can ask us to delete an enquiry you sent, and we will. Clinical records are kept for the period required of a chiropractic practice in California, and your file is kept for as long as your care and any related claim are open. For website form submissions, no retention period has been set yet.
The clause itself

Clinical records are kept for the period required of a chiropractic practice in California, and your file is kept for as long as your care and any related claim are open.

A retention period for website form submissions has not been set. It is one of the items being settled with counsel, and it is printed here as a number of months once it is. Meanwhile, you can ask us to delete an enquiry you sent, and we will.

3.8 Your rights in California

Plain answer
You can ask now, and this page cannot yet name the statutes you would be asking under. California rights over the personal information a business holds generally include asking what is held, asking for a copy, asking for a correction and asking for deletion. Which of them apply to this practice is still being confirmed with counsel. Ask using how to ask us about your information and we will handle the request, and you will not be treated differently for asking.
The clause itself

If you live in California you have rights over the personal information a business holds about you, which generally include asking what is held, asking for a copy, asking for it to be corrected, and asking for it to be deleted. Exactly which statutes apply to this practice, and in what way, is being confirmed with counsel, and this section names them once that is done.

You do not need to wait for that. Ask us for what you want using how to ask us about your information and we will handle the request. We will not treat you differently for asking.

3.9 Cookies and tracking

Plain answer
What the pages need to work, plus whatever the unconfirmed tag manager sets. That container is the same open question as 3.2. Your browser settings let you block or delete cookies, and blocking them will not stop you reading this site or reaching the practice by phone.
The clause itself

This site sets what is needed to make the pages work. Anything beyond that comes from the tag manager container described in 3.2, and it falls under the same open question. Your browser settings let you block or delete cookies, and blocking them will not stop you reading this site or reaching the practice by phone.

3.10 Children

Plain answer
It is not written for children, and the practice does not direct it at them or knowingly collect information from them through it. If a parent or guardian believes a child sent something through this site, call and we will remove it.
The clause itself

This site is written for adults dealing with a collision, and the practice does not direct it at children or knowingly collect information from them through it. If a parent or guardian believes a child sent something through this site, call and we will remove it.

3.11 Changes to this policy

Plain answer
The last reviewed date at the top moves whenever the policy does, and a change that is more than tidying is described here.
The clause itself

When this policy changes, the "last reviewed" date at the top changes with it. Where a change is more than tidying, this section says what changed and when.
