Why these fields, and not more

The date of the collision is the one that matters most: it lets us talk about the interval between the crash and your first examination the first time we speak, instead of a month later. The attorney question is asked so we know who to coordinate with, never as a condition of being seen. Everything else can wait. Stage 03 of 03. What it costs to ask

What it costs to ask

$0 out of pocket to start. You are billed nothing while your case is open, and we are paid from the settlement.

The terms behind that sentence, here rather than somewhere else:

  • The consultation. It is billed to your case like everything else. Nothing here is designated free, and we are not going to describe it that way.
  • What is billed, and when. The consultation, the examination, any imaging you agree to, and the care that follows. Payment comes out of the settlement when your case resolves. Not before, and not from you.
  • If the case is not accepted, is lost, or settles for less than the bill. The balance is written off in full and you owe nothing. All three end the same way.
  • Before we examine you, you can have a written estimate of what your care is expected to cost.

New auto-accident patients are seen the same day. That commitment covers new auto-accident patients only.
