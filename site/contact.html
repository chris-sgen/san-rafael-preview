Getting here, and what to bring

The clinic is on Mitchell Blvd in San Rafael, in Suite 10. Parking and building access details are being confirmed and will be published here rather than described from memory.

Bring, if you have them: photo ID, your insurance card, the police report or the exchange-of-information slip from the scene, your attorney's contact details, and the intake forms if you have already filled them in. The forms page has them to print or fill in beforehand. If you have none of that, come anyway. None of it is needed to be examined.

When not to come here first. If you have signs of a head injury, lost consciousness, have chest pain, have numbness that is getting worse, or you simply are not sure, go to an emergency department before you come to us. This practice is not an emergency room, not a medical doctor's office, and not a law firm. We examine and treat injuries, we document them, and we say so when something is outside what we can help with.

What happens after you get in touch

If you call during the hours above, you speak to the office and can arrange the visit on the call.

If you use the consultation form, it reaches the practice and someone replies to arrange the visit. We are not publishing a response-time promise, because the practice has not made one, and a stated reply window nobody has committed to is worse than no number at all. If you would rather not wait, call. The form is hosted by an outside vendor whose systems the practice does not yet control, which is also why we do not promise you an automatic confirmation message.
