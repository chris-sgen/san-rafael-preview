What he actually does when you come in after a crash

  1. 01

    He takes the history himself. How the collision happened, where you were sitting, where the headrest sat, which direction the impact came from, what hurt at the scene, and what hurt the next morning. Delayed onset is normal after a collision and the difference between those last two answers matters, so he asks for both.

  2. 02

    Then he examines you. Orthopedic tests, palpation, range of motion measured rather than estimated, and a neurological screen. What he finds is written down as objective findings, in numbers where numbers exist. A measurement taken on the day still says the same thing six months later. A note saying you felt sore does not.

  3. 03

    If the examination points to imaging, he says so out loud before anything is taken. X-rays are recommended only when the examination indicates them, they are optional and you may decline them, lead shielding is provided and offered without you having to ask for it, and the price is put in writing before the machine is switched on. Nothing is imaged to build a file.

  4. 04

    Then he writes the record. One person examines and one person authors it, so your file does not read as three clinicians disagreeing with each other.

  5. 05

    If care outside chiropractic is indicated, he says which kind of clinician you need and why. We describe that referral in our own words rather than implying a formal network, and we name no outside facility on this site.

What a chiropractor is not, and when he sends you elsewhere

Five bounds on this practice, drawn by the practice, with the destination attached to each.
What he is not Where that leaves you, and where to go instead
Not an emergency room. Head injury, loss of consciousness, numbness that is spreading, loss of bladder or bowel control, severe chest or abdominal pain: go to an emergency department first, then come to us.
Not a medical doctor's office. No prescriptions, no injections, no surgery. Where those are indicated he refers.
Not a law firm. He will speak to your attorney and supply the record. He will not advise you on your claim, and nothing on this site is legal advice.
Not a settlement service. Nothing here promises or estimates what a claim is worth. That is your attorney's territory and we stay out of it.
Not the right care for every injury. Where the injury is outside what conservative care can help, he tells you at the examination rather than starting a treatment plan. We can tell you whether we think we can help.
